Our Programs

There are many ways to get involved with SWS!

Seminar Series

Our seminar series is an intensive 10-week program held during Fall Quarter where students gain knowledge about financial markets, develop research and analytical skills, and learn investment fundamentals. During these weekly 1 hour seminars, industry professionals come in to speak to our members about various financial concepts. At the end of the ten weeks, participants will have the opportunity to apply all that they have learned through a guided research project. The seminar series is the starting point for those interested in joining SWS, but it is also a great opportunity for anyone who wants to learn more about investing!

Research Teams

After completing the seminar series, most new members join a research team as an Analyst. Our research teams focus on the Financial Institutions, Healthcare, Consumer, Media/Entertainment, Technology, and Energy industries. Research teams are composed of Senior Analysts and Analysts, who work together to deliver in-depth investment recommendations. They present their findings to the Investment Committee for consideration in our SWS Portfolio. In addition to conducting industry research, Analysts also attend more technical seminars on specific valuation methodologies, important financial metrics, and various other topics.

Investment Committee (ICOMM)

Once members have gained enough research experience as Senior Analysts, they are eligible to apply to become part of our SWS Investment Committee. The Investment Committee evaluates the investment recommendations presented by the research teams and decides whether they should be included in the SWS portfolio. This often involves more research on the particular stock and the industry, including conducting a DCF analysis. The Investment Committee is also responsible for providing regular portfolio updates, where they examine our current holdings and determine whether to hold or sell.

Professional Development Events

Every year, SWS hosts Women in Finance, the largest finance networking event for UChicago women. We also hold company site visits to Chicago-based firms and host sponsored stock pitch competitions with said firms. Companies we have partnered with include PEAK6 Investments, Rothschild & Co, and Greenhill & Co.

Our Events

We have many exciting events taking place each quarter!

Women in Finance Panel

Our annual Women in Finance Panel Event connects female undergraduates on campus to highly experienced and prominent women who have established their careers in the financial services industry. This event typically consists of a Keynote Address, Panel Discussion, workshops, and networking opportunities with our panel speakers and company representatives.

Poker Event

We collaborate with quantitative hedge funds, market makers, and proprietary trading firms to host women-only Poker Tournaments. Past tournaments:

  1. Peak6 PokerPower Workshop (reported on news by CBS)
  2. SIG-sponsored UChicago Womxn's Poker Tournament

Site Visits

We also offer site visits throughout the year for our members at companies. Past site visits have included:

  • Peak6 2024
  • Greenhill & Co. 2019
  • Lazard 2018
  • RMB Capital 2016
  • TD Ameritrade 2015

Our members also have access to events organized by Chicago Financial Women, a professional networking organization for finance professionals in Chicago.
